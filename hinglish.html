Step 1

Aapke saath kya hua?

Sabse milti-julti situation chuniye. Aapko turant karne wale kadam aur report karne ka tarika dikhega.

Step 2

Aaj kal ki sabse common thagiyan

Inke pattern pehchaan lenge to aadhi thagi wahin ruk jaati hai.

Sabse zyada khatarnak

"Digital arrest" call

Koi khud ko CBI, police ya customs batakar video call par "arrest" ka darr dikhata hai. Koi bhi agency video call par arrest ya paise ki maang nahi karti. Call kaat dijiye.

Naukri / task fraud

Ghar baithe kamayein

Pehle chhota payment milta hai bharosa banane ke liye, phir bade "deposit" maange jaate hain. Paisa wapas nahi aata.

UPI

Paisa lene ke liye PIN

PIN sirf paisa bhejte waqt daalna hota hai. Agar koi kehta hai "paisa aane ke liye PIN daalo" ya QR scan karo, to wo thagi hai.

Phishing

Fake KYC / bijli bill SMS

"Aapka account band ho jayega" jaisa dabav banakar nakli link bhejte hain. Bank ya company ka official app seedha kholiye, link nahi.

Sextortion

Video call par blackmail

Call record karke private video bhejne ki dhamki aur paise ki maang. Paise dene se maang badhti hai. Block kijiye, evidence rakhiye, report kijiye.

Customer care

Google par mila nakli number

Search results mein nakli helpline aa sakti hai. Number hamesha company ki official website ya app se lijiye.

Step 3

Report kahan karein

Sirf official sarkari ya recognised services. Ye website aapki taraf se koi complaint file nahi karti. Submit karne se pehle website ka address zaroor check karein.

Roz ki suraksha

Chhoti aadatein, bada bachaav

Koi technical knowledge nahi chahiye. Bas ye chhe cheezein.

OTP kisi ko nahi

Bank, company ya sarkari karamchari, koi bhi OTP nahi maangta.

Har account par alag password

Password manager ya lamba passphrase use kijiye. Ek leak sab khol deta hai.

2-step verification on

Gmail, WhatsApp, Instagram aur bank par. Password pata chal bhi jaye to account bachta hai.

Rukiye, phir socheyiye

Jaldi karne ka dabav hi thagi ka asli hathiyar hai. 10 minute rukkar kisi apne se poochiye.

Unknown APK install nahi

WhatsApp par aayi .apk file ya "update" link phone ka control de sakti hai.

Screenshot sambhaaliye

Chat, number, UPI ID aur transaction ID. Report mein ye sabse kaam aate hain.

Kya aap thagi pehchaan sakte hain?

Kuch aur jaanna hai?

Paisa kat gaya, sabse pehle kya karu?

Turant 1930 par call karein aur cybercrime.gov.in par complaint karein. Transaction ID, amount, time aur jis account mein paisa gaya uski detail taiyar rakhein. Apne bank ko bhi saath mein inform karein.

Kya ye website meri complaint file karti hai?

Nahi. Ye sirf jaankari aur official links deti hai. Complaint hamesha official portal par hi karein, aur yahan koi private photo ya document upload na karein.

Blackmail karne wale ko kuch paise de du to?

Nahi. Ek baar paisa dene par maang badhti jaati hai. Baat cheet band kijiye, account block karein, saboot rakhein aur report karein. Aap dosh nahi hain.

Kya report karne par police station jaana padega?

Zyadatar cases mein online complaint pehla kadam hoti hai. Aage ki process aur agar kisi jagah jaana pade, wo complaint ke baad aapko bataya jata hai.
