Step 2

The most common scams right now

Once you recognise the pattern, most scams stop right there.

Most dangerous

The "digital arrest" call

Someone claiming to be CBI, police or customs threatens you with "arrest" on a video call. No agency arrests people or demands money over video. Hang up.

Job and task fraud

Earn money from home

A small first payout builds trust, then larger "deposits" are demanded. The money never comes back.

UPI

"Enter your PIN to receive money"

A PIN is needed only to send money. If anyone asks for it to receive money, or asks you to scan a QR code, it is a scam.

Phishing

Fake KYC or electricity bill SMS

Pressure like "your account will be blocked" pushes you to a fake link. Open your bank's or provider's official app directly instead.

Sextortion

Blackmail after a video call

The call is recorded and you are threatened with leaks unless you pay. Paying only increases the demands. Block, save evidence and report.

Customer care

Fake number from a search

Fake helpline numbers can show up in search results. Always take the number from the company's official website or app.

Everyday safety

Small habits, big protection

No technical knowledge needed. Just these six things.

Never share an OTP

No bank, company or government officer will ever ask for it.

A different password everywhere

Use a password manager or a long passphrase. One leak should not open everything.

Turn on 2-step verification

On Gmail, WhatsApp, Instagram and your bank. Even a leaked password will not be enough.

Pause before you act

Pressure to hurry is the scammer's main weapon. Wait 10 minutes and ask someone you trust.

Never install unknown APKs

An .apk file or "update" link sent on WhatsApp can hand over control of your phone.

Keep screenshots

Chats, numbers, UPI IDs and transaction IDs are the most useful details in a report.

Frequently asked questions

Want to know more?

Money was debited. What do I do first?

Call 1930 immediately and file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Keep the transaction ID, amount, time and the receiving account ready. Also inform your bank.

Does this website file my complaint?

No. It only gives information and official links. Always file complaints on the official portal, and never upload private photos or documents here.

What if I pay the blackmailer a little?

Do not. Once you pay, the demands keep growing. Stop replying, block the account, keep the evidence and report it. It is not your fault.

Do I have to visit a police station to report?

In most cases an online complaint is the first step. If you need to go anywhere later, you will be told after the complaint is filed.
